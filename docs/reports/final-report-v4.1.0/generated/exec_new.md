- **New results in this report (Part I, seeds 400 to 409, 17 cells).** Global-CW OSCAR, one agent with one CW for all APs, improves on BEB by +15 % to +57 % in the 12 single-domain cells (2 to 4 APs, 20 to 100 stations, balanced or not), within 8.5 points of the best static CW in each. Independent per-AP OSCAR, trained here with a small budget, is below BEB in 16 of 16 cells. In the hidden chain (2C) no controller raises the starved middle BSS without lowering the total goodput: per-AP controllers (central online search, PF learner) raise it from 1.1 to 8.3 and 6.7 Mbit/s at -4.8 % and -5.8 % of total goodput. BEB with RTS/CTS is about 30 % below BEB in every saturated cell. Scenario 8 (uplink/downlink) and the OBSS-PD extension cannot be run in this simulator and are reported as such.
