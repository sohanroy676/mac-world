### Answers to the research questions

- **Does the learning algorithm converge as the number of APs increases?** The evidence is the frozen learner's performance and its spread over three independent trainings of the same budget (12 episodes of 15 s each), not training curves, which were not recorded in these runs. Global-CW OSCAR reaches +19.5 %, +36.4 %, +30.2 % and +51.9 % over BEB in 3A to 3D; the best common static CW gives +23.3 %, +44.9 %, +30.7 % and +54.4 %. The learner is therefore within 8.5 points of the best common CW at 3 APs and within 2.6 points at 4 APs. The largest gap between the three trainings in a cell is 2.99 Mbit/s of total goodput (table above for 3D).
- **Does the performance improvement remain consistent?** Yes for the controllers that read the whole channel: the gain over BEB grows with the total number of stations (30 in 3A, 40 in 3C, 60 in 3B, 80 in 3D; Global-CW OSCAR +19.5 % at 3x10 to +51.9 % at 4x20; DAC +23.1 % to +53.3 %). The reason is the baseline: BEB's goodput falls as stations are added (32.5 Mbit/s at 3x10, 25.6 at 4x20) while the tuned CW keeps the channel near its capacity. Independent per-AP OSCAR does not hold: -54.8 % in 3A to -73.9 % in 3D. KW is unreliable here (+18.9 % in 3A, -25.4 % in 3D).
- **Does the joint action space affect sample efficiency?** Not measurable from this data: the training budget was equal for 3 and 4 APs and the result at 4 APs is as good as at 3 relative to the optimum, so within this budget no loss is seen. Global-CW OSCAR has a single output at every size; only a per-AP actor (central critic prototype, Part II section 4.5) has an output that grows with the APs, and it was not part of this wave (it collapsed at 10 APs in the earlier study).
- **How does the learned CW distribution change with AP density?** Global-CW OSCAR's single CW is 313 slots at 3x10, 852 at 3x20, 346 at 4x10 and 740 at 4x20: it is smallest with 30 stations and larger with 40 and 60 (slightly smaller at 80 than at 60). The central online search rises steadily with the total number of stations (30, 40, 60, 80 stations: mean CW 115, 215, 377, 538 slots).

**Status:** answered for 3A to 3D with Global-CW OSCAR as the closest controller to the mentor's global Actor.
