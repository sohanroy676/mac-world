mac-world v4 plan: one controller that is near-optimal in every scenario

The final plan for the rest of the project. Built on the v3.3.0 to v3.4.2 results, with every assumption checked against the code on dev 33708ff. Bottom line

  1. Where every AP hears every other, a 2005 heuristic already matches OSCAR. At 20,40, IdleSense with the published rule and no learning gives 38.77 ± 0.21 Mbps against Global-CW OSCAR's 38.65 ± 0.40 (n=8, p = 0.31). That is +40 % over BEB and close to the best static CW of 39.62 fact. No learner can claim a real aggregate win here.
  2. In the flow-in-the-middle chain, every baseline fails on one side. IdleSense has the highest aggregate (78.12) but starves the middle AP to 0.53 Mbps. Global-CW leaves it at 2.6 Mbps. Static per-AP CWs are fair only after a per-scenario search. A PF-reward learner reaches the static oracle's PF, but how close it gets depends on the training seed fact.
  3. So the contribution is robustness across scenarios, not one more win in one of them. The headline metric is the share of the BEB-to-oracle gap each method closes in every scenario and at every fairness level. The claim: one trained controller, never retrained, is never meaningfully worse than the best baseline in any cell, and beats each baseline clearly in at least one, with every method held to the same fair floor so no AP is starved. IdleSense, Global-CW, OSCAR and the static settings each have a scenario where they collapse inference, partly fact.
  4. The method is simplified to the parts that survived testing (working name RAFA): one shared DDPG actor fed by the AP's own idle-slot, retry and load readings; a fairness reward weighted by station count, with the fairness level as an input; a fair floor against BEB; training over a fixed pool of randomised scenarios; and 3-seed ensembles at deployment. The analytic-prior residual, TD3 and the backoff-mode action are dropped, because v3.3 and v3.4 showed no stable benefit from them fact.
  5. The largest risk is variance between training seeds, not compute. One-seed rankings flipped three times in v3.4.0 fact. So every comparison uses 3 training seeds, and RAFA is trained as 6 seeds forming two independent 3-seed ensembles; every claim must hold for both.

The claim and how it is scored

PROBLEM

Constrained by a fair floor

No AP may drop below floor_i = min(BEB_i, BEB aggregate · n_i / Σn). A result that breaks the floor in a cell is infeasible there. It is still reported, but cannot win that cell.

METRIC

Gap closed (GC)

GC = (U_α − U_α,BEB) / (U_α,oracle − U_α,BEB) for α ∈ {0, 1, 2}, with U_α = Σ n_i·u_α(max(ε, x_i/n_i)). BEB scores 0 and the best feasible static setting scores 1. Delay cells use GC_D on log p99. Small-gap cells use the raw metric (see Statistics).

CLAIM 1

Never meaningfully worse

In every core cell, RAFA is not more than 0.05 GC below the best feasible baseline (intersection-union test, each cell at 0.05).

CLAIM 2

Every rival beaten somewhere

For each baseline, at least one cell where RAFA is significantly better (paired t, Holm over all cell × baseline pairs).

Headline: mean and worst-cell GC for every method in one table, both RAFA ensembles shown. RAFA does not need to win the one-domain cells, where IdleSense ties the ceiling. It needs to be close there and clearly better where each rival breaks: IdleSense in the chain (infeasible, because it starves the middle AP), Global-CW at α ≥ 1, static settings and per-scenario learners in dynamic and unseen scenarios, and BEB everywhere. Aggregate goodput, per-AP throughput, Jain, delay and collision rate are reported for every cell, and unconstrained results are shown next to the constrained ones.

Baselines

Every baseline runs on the same seeds. Per-station rules are run at AP level (the AP sets its BSS's CW, as OSCAR does) and labelled as such. Each gets the same tuning budget, 3 to 4 settings of its main parameter on selection seeds 0 to 2, never on evaluation seeds. Each adaptive baseline gets a convergence check on selection seeds; if one has not settled by the 3 s window start, every method in that cell runs for 30 s. PI, UCB, CCOD and BLADE-style run on S2 and S4 only; the main rivals run in every core cell.

Statistics

  • Seeds: selection python seeds 0 to 2 (ns-3 1 to 3) for tuning, oracle choice and the episode count. Evaluation python seeds 100 to 109 (ns-3 101 to 110), reusing the v3.4.0 and v3.3.1 runs on 100 to 107. New training uses ns-3 seeds 2001 and up, disjoint from both.
  • Training seeds: RAFA 6 (two ensembles). PF learner and drlCW 3 each. Global-CW 3 on S2 and S4 (revision 8) and 1 elsewhere; OSCAR per AP and CCOD 1 each, with online OSCAR shown as published.
  • Two stages: Stage 1 runs every method on seeds 100 to 102 and ranks them per cell; it is reported in full. Stage 2 adds seeds 103 to 109 for both RAFA ensembles, the top 3 feasible baselines in each cell, and each baseline in its weakest cell against RAFA. Stage-1 runs are reused.
  • Tests: paired t on per-seed differences is primary; the exact Wilcoxon is reported beside it, unadjusted and descriptive only (at n = 10 its smallest p is about 0.002). Claim 1 is an intersection-union non-inferiority test with margin 0.05 GC, every cell at 0.05. Claim 2 tests each baseline in one cell picked from Stage 1, using only the fresh seeds 103 to 109, with Holm over baselines. Power rule: a cell under 80 % power at a true tie with n = 10 gets n = 20 (seeds 110 to 119); if it is still under 80 %, it is reported as inconclusive and is not a pass. Both RAFA ensembles must pass.
  • Edge cases fixed in advance: per-station ε = 0.01 Mbps inside u_α. A cell is degenerate when U_oracle − U_BEB is below 3 seed SDs of BEB; it is then scored on the raw metric with a 1 % margin. Delay cells use GC_D on log p99, with the oracle as the static CW with the lowest p99 at ≥ 99 % delivered load.
  • Pre-registration: an analysis spec (metric code, margins, cells, seeds, tests) is committed before M4 starts.
  • Report: the GC table (method × cell, mean and worst, feasible and unconstrained), wins / ties / losses, Pareto plots of aggregate against min-AP for S4 to S6, delay CDFs for S7, and S8 time series with regret against the switching oracle.

Decisions taken

You delegated these. Each is recorded here and in the repo and can be reversed.

  1. Claim: robustness across the suite under a fair floor (GC, Claims 1 and 2), not a win in one scenario. In one domain IdleSense already ties the ceiling, so no other honest large claim is available.
  2. Method: RAFA as above, with DDPG. Residual prior, TD3 and the backoff-mode action are dropped on the v3.3 and v3.4 evidence.
  3. Fairness: station-weighted α-fair utility with ε, and a station-weighted fair floor that applies to every method.
  4. Variance: 6 RAFA seeds in two ensembles, 3 seeds for the main learned rivals, n=10 evaluation seeds, pre-registered analysis.
  5. Scope: per-STA on/off traffic yes (ADR-0006). Shared actor with team reward as the main method (ADR-0007). Neighbour summaries only as the RAFA-N variant.
  6. Compute: current machine, lanes fixed first, lower-load test, trim order.
  7. Baselines: re-implemented ones are labelled as such and built only from texts that were read; each gets equal tuning and a convergence check.
